step "Grain ready"
ask "Have the grain (" + rcp_Total_Grain_Weight + " " + rcp_Weight_Units + ") milled and ready to add. Press Done when it is ready."
step "Strike water to the mash tun"
ask "Move " + rcp_Strike_Volume + " " + rcp_Volume_Units + " of strike water from the Hot Liquor Tank into the mash tun. Press Done when it is in."
@if Mash_Pump
ask "Set the valves so the mash pump recirculates the mash tun (through the HERMS coil or RIMS tube). Press Done when they are set."
{{Mash_Pump}} = true
@end
step "Dough in"
ask "Add the grain slowly: add, stir, add, stir, and stir out every dough ball with the mash paddle. Press Done when it is all in."
@if HLT_Heater
{{HLT_Heater}}.target = rcp_Sparge_Temp
@end
step "Vorlauf"
ask "Recirculate the first cloudy runnings back on top of the mash until they run clear: this sets the grain bed. Press Done when it runs clear."
@if Mash_Probe
print "Mash temperature after dough in: " + {{Mash_Probe}} + rcp_Temp_Units
@end
