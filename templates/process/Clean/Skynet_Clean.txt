step "Rinse"
all_pumps_off
ask "Rinse the vessels, hoses and chiller with water to get the grain, hops and trub out. Press Done when they are rinsed."
step "Clean"
ask "Fill the vessel you clean from with hot water and cleaner (the cleaner's label says how much and how hot). Press Done when it is ready."
@if Clean_Pump
ask "Set the valves and hoses so the cleaning pump sends the cleaner through the vessels, pipes and chiller and back. Press Done when they are set."
{{Clean_Pump}} = true
sleep 1200000
{{Clean_Pump}} = false
@else
ask "Scrub the vessels and run the cleaner through the pipes, pump and chiller. Press Done when it is all clean."
@end
step "Rinse again"
ask "Drain the cleaner and rinse everything with clean water. Press Done when it is rinsed."
step "Leave it to dry"
all_pumps_off
ask "Open the drains and turn off any tap water. Press Done when you are done."
step "Open all valves"
open_all_valves
print "Clean done: every valve on the Open List is open to dry (never a tap water valve)"
