# Brew Pump Pictures

- **A Hose or Pipe from the real ports.** The pump's **IN** and **OUT** IPs are on two of its 8 IP spots (Help 130), and a Hose or Pipe is drawn from the tip of its real inlet and outlet ports to them. Orientation Left mirrors the picture, the Hose or Pipe and the start spots.
- **All in the same box (the Pump Standard, Help 130):** every pump picture is drawn to scale in the same 300 x 300 box and every pump has its 8 IPs in the same spots, so one pump can replace another in the same place and stay joined. Some port tubes are drawn a little longer to line up.
- **Center inlet** pumps are drawn from the side: motor on the left, inlet pointing right out of the middle of the head, outlet pointing up.
- **90° inlet** (in-line head) pumps are drawn looking at the head: the inlet comes out the side, at 90° to the outlet, which points up.
- Polysulfone heads are drawn amber (they are see-through amber on the real pump), stainless heads light grey, plastic heads black.
- Drawn to scale: 10 drawing units = 1 inch.
