# Brew Pump Pictures

Skynet has drawings of the common homebrew pumps, drawn from the real pumps with **no cover**. Pick one as a Pump Device's **Picture** (Pumps group). Only head styles that are really sold were drawn.

- **The IPs sit on the real ports.** With one of these pictures, the pump's **IN** IP is on the tip of the inlet port and its **OUT** IP on the tip of the outlet port, where the hose or pipe goes on. Flip Horizontal and Flip Vertical move the IPs with the picture.
- **All in the same box (the Pump Standard, Help 130):** every pump picture is drawn to scale in the same 300 x 300 box with its IN and OUT IPs in the same spots, so one pump can replace another in the same place and stay joined. Some port tubes are drawn a little longer to reach the spots.
- **Center inlet** pumps are drawn from the side: motor on the left, inlet pointing right out of the middle of the head, outlet pointing up.
- **90° inlet** (in-line head) pumps are drawn looking at the head: the inlet comes out the side, at 90° to the outlet, which points up.
- Polysulfone heads are drawn amber (they are see-through amber on the real pump), stainless heads light grey, plastic heads black.
- Drawn to scale: 10 drawing units = 1 inch.
