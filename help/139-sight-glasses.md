# Sight Glasses

A **Sight Glass** lets you see the wort or water going through a pipe. Skynet has two, both **TC Pipe Fittings** (Skynet Rule 5.32,
see the TC Pipe Fittings page):

| Sight Glass | What it looks like |
| --- | --- |
| **Sight Glass** | a plain glass tube between two TC 1.5 ferrules |
| **Sight Glass Caged** | the same, with a stainless cage round the glass |

Each has an **IP on the outer face of each ferrule** (left and right), so a pipe joins each end. Flow goes either way through it.

## Adding one

In **Edit layout**, pick **Elbows, Tees … > Sight Glass** (or Sight Glass Caged), or **Add Widget > TC Pipe Fittings**. It goes on
the Build Tab like every new item (Rule 4.1), 6 inches long. Its **Label** starts as **site_glass_1**, **site_glass_2** and so on (you can change it,
but it can't be blank).

Drop it on a straight pipe and Skynet cuts the pipe and puts the sight glass in, the same as a tee.

## Making it longer or shorter: Length

Double-click it to open its Properties. **Length** is in inches, ferrule face to ferrule face (2 to 48). Type a new Length and
press **Save**: only the **glass** (and the cage) gets longer or shorter. The ferrules keep their size and shape.

- **Autosize to pipe** (always on) keeps it as thick as the pipe joined to it.

Turn it upright with the turn buttons on the Edit layout bar. Press **Save layout** to keep your changes.
