# The Pump Standard: swap one pump for another

Every Skynet pump picture is drawn in the **same 300 x 300 box**, with its **inlet and outlet IPs in the same spots** (Skynet Rule
5.27). So you can take one pump out, put a different one in the **same X and Y at the same size**, and it stays joined to the same
pipes.

## Where the IPs are

A pump starts at **150 x 150** on a Tab. With Orientation **Right**:

| IP | In the 300 x 300 drawing | On a 150 x 150 pump |
|----|-----|-----|
| Inlet (IN) | 285 across, 150 down | 142.5 across, 75 down (the middle of its height, near the right side) |
| Outlet (OUT) | 243 across, 56 down | 121.5 across, 28 down (near the top right) |

With Orientation **Left** the picture and both IPs are mirrored, so the inlet is near the left side.

If you resize a pump, the spots grow or shrink with its box. Two pumps of the **same size** always have their IPs in the same place.

## Still drawn to scale

The brew pump drawings (March, Chugger, Blichmann Riptide, Topsflo, KegLand) are all at the same scale: 1 inch is 20 in the 300 x 300
box, or 10 on a 150 x 150 pump. Pumps with ports closer together have a **longer straight port tube** drawn on, so the tips reach the
spots. The Covers line up the same way. The older Riptide-style color pictures follow the Pump Standard too.

## Replacing a pump

1. Open **Edit layout** and note the old pump's **X** and **Y** (and its size, 150 x 150 to start).
2. Add the new pump with the same Orientation and put it at the same X and Y (or delete the old one first).
3. Its inlet and outlet land on the same pipe ends. **Save layout**.

## Older Schemas

The first time a Schema is opened, each Pump (and each Picture showing a Skynet pump) becomes 150 x 150 about its middle, and it is
moved back onto its pipes (Rule 5.23).

The **sump pump** sits in the liquid and draws in through its screen, so it keeps its own size. A pump with your own picture keeps
its IPs in the middle of its sides.
