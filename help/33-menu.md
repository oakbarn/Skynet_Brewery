# The ☰ Menu

The **☰ Menu** button is at the top right of the **Tabs** page, where the Edit layout box used to be. Press it to see its choices. (The top line of the panel with Processes, Settings, Help ... is the Menu Bar, see [The Menu Bar](40-menu-bar).)

- **Show Menu Bar** / **Hide Menu Bar**: the top line of the panel is hidden on the Tabs page; this brings it back. See [The Menu Bar](40-menu-bar).
- **Edit layout** (Admins): changes the tabs. It is the first choice. While you are in Edit layout it says **Exit Edit layout**. See [Tabs](02-tabs).
- The **Menu tabs**: Zoom (number 1 to start with), Default, Equipment Profile, Vessel, Vessel Templates, Equipment Templates, Fitting Templates, Default Layout, Mash Profile, Boil Profile, Tab Background Profile and Valve Profile.
- **File Move Utility** (Admins) opens the File Move page. See [File Move](32-file-move).
- **Recipe** and **Fermentation** are greyed out. They are coming in the next stages.
- **Schema**: the Schema dropdown (the same one as on the Menu Bar), every Schema on the Brain with **Load** and **Clone**. See [Switching and cloning Schemas](47-schema-switching-and-cloning).
- **About** (at the end): who is signed in, **Sign out**, the Schema dropdown and the Schema version. See [About and Schema versions](42-about-and-schema-versions).

## Menu tabs

A Menu tab only shows once you pick it. It opens next to your screen tabs, with a ☰ in front of its name, and fills the space where the tab drawing is.

- Press a screen tab (Brewery, Equipment ...) to go back to your tabs. The Menu tabs you opened stay up there, so one press takes you back.
- Press the **✕** on a Menu tab to close it.
- Every Menu tab also has **Close** and a boxed **✕** at its top right corner. Either one closes the Menu tab you are looking at. The Help page and the File Move page have them too; there they take you back to where you were.
- The Menu Bar always shows on a Menu tab (only your own Tabs hide it, see [The Menu Bar](40-menu-bar)).
- If a Menu tab has changes that are not saved, the panel asks before closing it or switching away.

Everyone can look at the Menu tabs. Only an Admin can change them (an Operator can lock and unlock the Equipment Profile).

## The Default tab

**Every default is set here.** Whether the Menu Bar is hidden on Tabs (see [The Menu Bar](40-menu-bar)). What a new Recipe starts with: the Units, the Batch size, the Equipment Profile, the Mash Profile and the Boil Profile (each one can be changed in the Recipe itself). The Tab Background Profile a new tab gets, and the Valve Profile picked first for a new valve. **On startup**: the Zoom and Full screen the panel starts with (see [Zoom and Full screen](41-zoom-and-full-screen)). **Large text size** is how big `\L` large text in a Process string is (more than 18, see [Codes inside text](22-process-language)). **Text**: the Defaults of every text box (Visible, Fore color, Back color, Alignment, Word wrap, Name font and Name font size, and the size and place of new items, see [Text boxes](47-text-boxes)). Whether **phones and tablets can move and resize things** in Edit layout (off at first, see [Phones and tablets](45-phones-and-tablets)). Press **Save**.

**Sound** (at the top of the Default tab and under the defaults):

- **Sound on**: the same as the sound button at the top of the page. It turns sound on or off on this screen right away, and is not saved.
- **Turn Sound On when starting**: when this is on, sound is on by itself every time the panel opens and every time a Schema is loaded, so you do not have to press **Enable sound**. Press **Save** to keep it.

Browsers (Chrome, Edge, Safari, iPhone and Android) do not let a page play any sound until you tap or click it once. So when the panel opens, the button at the top says **Sound on (tap anywhere)**: tap or click anywhere on the page once and the sound plays from then on. An alarm that went off before that first tap starts as soon as you tap.

**Order of the Menu:** each Menu tab has an Order number. Type a new number to move a Menu tab there. The others are numbered again to make room (1, 2, 3 ...), and the Menu and the open Menu tabs sort themselves. New Menu tabs added later go at the end.

## The other Menu tabs

- **Zoom**: how big the tabs show, and Full screen. See [Zoom and Full screen](41-zoom-and-full-screen).
- **Equipment Profile**: the losses and efficiency, the vessels, and which profile each Tab of the loaded Schema uses. See [Schemas and Equipment Profiles](28-schemas-and-equipment-profiles).
- **Vessel**: one vessel (or chiller, coil, filter) at a time. See [The Vessel tab](34-vessel-tab).
- **Vessel Templates**, **Equipment Templates** and **Fitting Templates**: ready-made vessels, chillers, coils, trub filters and Bundled Fittings (Skynet and your own) as small icons. Double-click one for its settings, or put a copy on a Tab. See [Vessel and Fitting Templates](50-vessel-and-fitting-templates).
- **Default Layout**: the vessels and pipes a Tab gets with its Equipment Profile. See [Schemas and Equipment Profiles](28-schemas-and-equipment-profiles).
- **Mash Profile** and **Boil Profile**: see [Mash and Boil Profiles](35-mash-and-boil-profiles).
- **Tab Background Profile** and **Valve Profile**: see [Tab Background and Valve Profiles](36-tab-and-valve-profiles).
- **Schema**: see [Switching and cloning Schemas](47-schema-switching-and-cloning).
- **About**: see [About and Schema versions](42-about-and-schema-versions).
