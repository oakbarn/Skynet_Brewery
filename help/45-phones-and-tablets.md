# Phones and tablets

**A phone or a tablet is for running and watching the brewery, not for editing or building** (Rule 2.35). Edit and build on a computer (Windows, Mac or Linux) with a mouse and keyboard.

## Run mode: what works on a phone or tablet

When an admin signs in on a phone or tablet, the panel works the way it does for an operator:

- **Change the Schema** with the Schema picker on the Menu Bar.
- **Start, Stop and Restart Processes**, and **Stop all**.
- **The buttons on the Tabs**: pumps, valves, switches, Override buttons, Timers and the rest work with a tap.
- Watching the Tabs, alarms, the Log and the Simulator bar.

## What is not on a phone or tablet

- The **Edit layout** button (and the ☰ Menu > Build screens).
- **Properties** and **Tab settings** (double-click or holding a finger does not open them).
- The admin screens, such as **Schema.json**, **File Move** and the admin parts of Settings.

The panel shows a short note when it starts in run mode, and **☰ Menu > About** says "run mode on this phone or tablet" next to who is signed in. Nothing is changed on the Brain: your login is still an admin login, and on a computer it works as before.

## How the panel knows

The browser tells the panel whether the screen is used with a finger only (a phone or tablet) or with a mouse or trackpad (a computer). This works on iPhone, iPad, Android and computers. An iPad that asks for the "desktop" web site still counts as a tablet.

A laptop with a touch screen counts as a computer, because it also has a mouse or trackpad. An iPad with a keyboard and trackpad may count as a computer too.

**☰ Menu > Default** shows what the screen you are on counts as.

## Editing on a phone or tablet anyway

On a computer, open **☰ Menu > Default > Phones and tablets**, switch on **Phones and tablets can edit** and press **Save**. It is for every phone and tablet. Then reload the panel on the phone or tablet.

With it on, Edit layout on a phone or tablet still does **not move or resize anything**, so a finger can't drag a pump or a pipe away by accident: tap an item to pick it, hold a finger on it for its properties, and change **X**, **Y**, **Width** and **Height** there. The second switch, **Phones and tablets can move and resize things in Edit layout**, lets them move too.
