# Phones and tablets

On a phone or a tablet, **Edit layout does not move or resize anything.** A finger that slides across the screen can't drag a pump or a pipe away by accident.

## What still works on a phone or tablet

- **Tap** an item to pick it. **🔒 Lock** / **🔓 Unlock** works on the picked item.
- **Hold a finger** on an item to open its properties. To move it a little, change **X** and **Y** there; to resize it, change **Width** and **Height**.
- Draw pipes and hoses, add elements, graphics, text, equipment and IPs.
- **⚙ Tab settings** (or hold a finger on an empty spot).

The resize corners and the bend points of hoses are not shown on a phone or tablet.

On a computer with a mouse nothing changes: drag to move, drag a corner to resize.

## How the panel knows

The browser tells the panel whether the screen is used with a finger only (a phone or tablet) or with a mouse or trackpad (a computer). This works on iPhone, iPad, Android and computers. An iPad that asks for the "desktop" web site still counts as a tablet.

A laptop with a touch screen counts as a computer, because it also has a mouse or trackpad. An iPad with a keyboard and trackpad may count as a computer too.

**☰ Menu > Default** shows what the screen you are on counts as.

## Moving things on a phone or tablet anyway

**☰ Menu > Default > Phones and tablets**: switch on **Phones and tablets can move and resize things in Edit layout** and press **Save**. It is for every phone and tablet. Leave Edit layout and open it again to use it.
