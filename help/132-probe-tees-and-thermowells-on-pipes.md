# Probe tees and thermowells on pipes

Tees with a Temperature Probe and Thermowells are **Temperature Devices** with **IPs**, so pipes and hoses join them like any
fitting (Skynet Rule 5.30).

## Tees with a Temperature Probe

The **Instrument Tee** and **Long Tee** with a probe (in the branch, or in from the end) are the **same size as the TC Pipe Fittings
Tee and Instrument Tee**, and their IPs are on the **same spots**: the middle of each open ferrule face. The probe fills one opening,
so a probe tee has two IPs. You can put a probe tee where a tee was and it lines up.

In its Properties: **Flip Horizontal**, **Flip Vertical** and **Rotate** (0, 90, 180, 270). The picture and its IPs follow.

### Dropping one on a pipe

In **Edit layout**, drag a probe tee (with no pipes on it yet) onto a straight pipe and let go:

- **Probe in the branch:** its two open ends go along the pipe (it turns if it was across the pipe), and the pipe is **cut in two**,
  one piece on each end. This is the same as a normal Tee (Rule 5.25).
- **Probe in from the end:** its open end goes on the pipe, and its branch is taken round to the rest of the pipe with **90 degree
  elbows**, as for a pump.
- Dropped over an **open end** of a pipe, the pipe is made **shorter** and joined to it.
- It then takes the **pipe's size**, the same as a TC Pipe Fitting.

## Tees on hoses

A **Tee**, **Instrument Tee** or **Cross** (TC Pipe Fittings), or a **probe tee**, dropped on a **hose** cuts the hose. A hose
bends, so the tee stays where you dropped it and each piece of hose goes to one end of the tee's run. A **Quick Disconnect hose** and a
🔒 locked hose are never cut. Pumps and valves don't cut hoses.

## Thermowells

A **Thermowell** has **one IP**, on the face of its TC cap (the end that clamps on). Nothing flows through it: it fills the opening it
is in. It can **Rotate** and **Flip** like a probe tee.

In **Edit layout**, drop a Thermowell with its cap near another IP (within 30 px), such as a **tee's branch** or a **vessel port**,
and it **snaps on**: its IP sits on that IP.

### On a front facing IP

A **front facing IP** is an opening you look straight into: a port on the **front of a vessel**, or the opening of a **front view**
valve or elbow. A Thermowell dropped there shows **from the front**: the round TC cap with the probe (purple) in its middle. Drag it off
and it shows from the side again.

A **plain Temperature Probe** dropped with its middle on a front facing IP has a Thermowell there too, so it changes into a
**Thermowell seen from the front**. Drag it off and it is the plain probe it was.
