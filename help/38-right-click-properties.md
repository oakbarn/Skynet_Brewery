# Right-click for Properties

Every item on a tab has a **Properties** dialog: Devices, Widgets, vessels, IPs and fittings, pipes and hoses, pictures and text. In **Edit layout** you can open it with a **right-click**.

## How it works

1. Press **☰ Menu** and pick **✎ Edit layout** (Admins only). The tab is now "unlocked" for changes.
2. **Right-click** an item. Its Properties dialog opens (the same one a double-click opens), with **Delete**, **Cancel** and **Save** at the top and bottom.
3. **Right-click an empty spot** on the tab, or right-click a **Tab button** at the top, to open **⚙ Tab settings** for that tab.

## When right-click does nothing special

- **Edit layout is off.** On brew day the tab is locked against changes, so right-click shows the browser's normal menu (or nothing on a push button). Nothing can be changed by accident.
- **The item is 🔒 locked in place.** Right-click selects it and says it is locked. Press **🔓 Unlock** in the Edit layout bar, then right-click it again. (A double-click still opens a locked item, so you can switch **Lock position** off there too.)
- **You are drawing a pipe or hose.** Finish or cancel the pipe first.

## Bundles

A vessel that is **📦 Bundled** with its IPs opens its short Bundled dialog (Bundled and Tab). Right-clicking one of its IPs opens that same vessel dialog. Switch **Bundled** off to edit the vessel or its IPs one by one. See [Equipment](14-equipment).

## Phones and tablets

Touch screens have no right-click. **Hold a finger** on the item for about half a second instead; on an empty spot that opens **⚙ Tab settings**.

## Mac

On a Mac, a right-click is a **Control-click**, or a two-finger click on the trackpad.

See also [Tabs](02-tabs).
