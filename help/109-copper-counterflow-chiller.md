# Copper Counterflow Chiller

Fritz, 2026-11-02: the **Skynet Copper Counterflow Chiller** is a chiller Widget made from his Copper_CounterFlow drawing. It is a copper coil: the wort runs in the inner tube and the cold water in the outer tube, the opposite way, and the two never mix.

## Its 4 IPs

The chiller comes with **4 built-in IPs**, one on the tip of each real port of the picture. Every port faces left, so a pipe joins each one **Horizontally** (pipes run only Horizontal, Vertical or 45°).

| Port | Where it is on the picture | Side |
|---|---|---|
| **Wort In** | Top, the end that runs straight through the top tee | Wort |
| **Water Out** | Top, the short branch off the top tee | Water |
| **Water In** | Bottom, the short branch off the bottom tee | Water |
| **Wort Out** | Bottom, the end that runs straight through the bottom tee | Wort |

Hot wort goes in at the top and comes out cold at the bottom; tap water goes in at the bottom and leaves warm at the top. Flow passes from Wort In to Wort Out and from Water In to Water Out only.

Draw a pipe to an IP and its end snaps on (IP snap, 10 px). The IPs stay on the port tips when you move, resize, flip or rotate the chiller.

## Adding one

1. Open the **🔨 Build Tab** (new Widgets are made there first).
2. ☰ Menu > **Equipment Templates**, pick **Copper_Counterflow_Chiller** and put a copy on the Build Tab (or **Add equipment ➕** on the Edit layout bar).
3. Pipe it up, then **Save Build** to send it to its Tab.

It is a Skynet Template: its picture and port places are set (fixed). Its Type is **Plate chiller, Counterflow** with **MPT 1/2"** ports, the same as the other counterflow chillers.

The picture is `Images/Skynet/Chillers/Chiller_Copper_Counterflow.svg`, redrawn as plain SVG (Skynet pictures are SVG only).
