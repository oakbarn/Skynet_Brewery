# IPs line up by themselves

Pipes join at **IPs** (the round connection points). Two things make it easy to get them right.

## The IPs sit where the water flows

On a Skynet valve picture (motorized ball valve, manual ball valve, butterfly valve, gas solenoid valve) the two IPs sit on the
picture's pipe ends, exactly where the water goes in and out. They are no longer in the middle of the box's sides.

- A **horizontal** valve has its IPs on the left and right ends of its pipe.
- A **vertical** valve has its IPs on the top and bottom ends of its pipe, on the motor's (or handle's) other side.
- Make the valve bigger or smaller and its IPs stay on the pipe ends.

## Snap to align

In **Edit layout**, drag something that has IPs (a valve, a pump, a fitting, a vessel or an IP). When one of its IPs comes
within **10 pixels** of an IP of something else on the Tab (a pipe end, a fitting, a valve, a vessel port), it **jumps** so the two
IPs sit exactly on each other. Let go and they are lined up.

Pipes joined to the item still follow it while it moves, as before.

**Hold Alt** while you drag to place it without the jump.
