# Every screen in the ☰ Menu

The **☰ Menu** lists every place in the panel you can go to, so nothing is hidden. It is the same list everywhere: the ☰ Menu on the Tabs page, the ☰ on the Menu Bar of the other pages, and the small ☰ in the corner of a window (see [The ☰ Menu everywhere](51-menu-everywhere)).

The list has three parts:

1. **Show Menu Bar** and **Edit layout** (Admins).
2. **Menu tabs**: Zoom, Default, Equipment Profile, Vessel, the Templates, the Profiles, Schema, About ... in their Order (see [The ☰ Menu](33-menu)).
3. **Pages**: every page of the Menu Bar, and under each page, marked with **↳**, its parts.

Pick a page to go to its top. Pick a **↳** part to go straight to it: the page opens, scrolls to that part, and lights it up for a moment. A part that is a window (Recycle Bin, Backups ...) opens that window.

## The parts under each page

- **Tabs**: each of your screen Tabs (the one you are on is in bold).
- **Processes**: Find in all Processes, Recycle Bin (deleted Processes), Color key.
- **Variables**: gAPI, gKonstant, Shared variables.
- **Log**: Download CSV, Show readings.
- **Devices**: **Interfaces (boards)** (the boards (PLCs) and their Interface IDs), Add Interface, OneWire probe index, OneWire probes seen.
- **Import**: BeerSmith recipe, BruControl Schema.
- **Settings**: Start from a sample Schema, My account (password), Sign-in codes by email or text, Users, Password rules, Recovery code, Sending codes, Testing mode, Simulation, Panel settings, Beer money pop-up, Pictures (PNG / JPG to SVG), MQTT and voice.
- **Schema.json**: Backups.
- **Media**, **File Move** and **Help** have no parts: the whole page is one screen.

## Who sees what

A page or part that only Admins can use (File Move, Schema.json, Users, MQTT and voice ...) only shows in the ☰ Menu of an Admin, the same as on the page itself. Operators and Viewers see the rest.

The Brew Day Tabs stay clean: these choices live in the ☰ Menu, one press away.
