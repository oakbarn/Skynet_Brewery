# Patron

Skynet is free. A **Patron** helps pay for it, and gets a few extras (Skynet Rule 12.4).

## What a Patron gets

**Patron: $10 every 6 months**

- **Request a Feature**: ask the OakBarn Brewery team for a new feature, straight from **☰ Menu > ⭐ Request a Feature**.
- **Email alert for new Releases**: an email when a new Skynet comes out. You sign up on oakbarnbrewery.com; OakBarn Brewery sends it, never your Brain.
- **Send your pump to OakBarn Brewery** (coming): a pump you made can become a Skynet pump that every brewer gets.
- **Your Brewery Name**: the name you type in **☰ Menu > Default settings > Brewery Name** (empty: the name on your Patron code) shows on every Tab's Menu Bar and in the Process Editor, and every new Process says **Created by** your Brewery Name with the MIT License line and **Copyright ©** your Brewery Name. Not a Patron: the Menu Bar shows **Skynet Panel**, the Brewery Name box is greyed out (what you typed is kept), and a new Process has no Created by, MIT License or Copyright line (Skynet did not write it; you can type your own).

The levels and the extras may change as OakBarn Brewery sees how they work.

## Your Patron code

When you become a Patron, OakBarn Brewery emails you a **Patron code**. It starts with `SKYPATRON1.` and is one long line.

1. Open **Settings > Patron** (an Admin).
2. Paste the whole code in **Patron code** and tap **Save Patron code**.
3. **Settings > Patron** now shows ⭐ **Patron** and the end date, and the extras are unlocked until that day.

Renewing? Paste the new code the same way. **Remove Patron code** locks the extras again.

Your Brain checks that the code really comes from OakBarn Brewery: a code that was made up, or with one letter changed, is refused.

## Your email address stays with you

A Patron code holds only the name to show, the level and the end date: **never an email address**. Patron emails are sent by OakBarn Brewery from outside Skynet, so your Brain never holds them. OakBarn Brewery never sells or shares an email address, never sends spam, and **never emails you about your password** (see [Login, users and passwords](10-login)).

## Request a Feature

**☰ Menu > ⭐ Request a Feature**: type a short title and what it should do, then tap **Send to OakBarn Brewery**.

- If your Brain sends emails (**Settings > Sending codes**), it is sent from your own email account to support@oakbarnbrewery.com.
- If not, your own mail program opens with the request filled in: press **Send** there.

Not a Patron yet? It shows what the Patron extras are, with a link to oakbarnbrewery.com.
