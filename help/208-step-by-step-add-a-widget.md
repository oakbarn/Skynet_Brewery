# Step by Step: Add a Widget

A **Widget** lives only in the app: it is not wired to a board, so it has no Interface and no Port. Pictures, text, switches, timers, alarms, fittings and the Manual Valve are Widgets. This guide adds a **Hose Bib** (the brass outside faucet, a Tap Water Valve Widget) called **Garden hose** to the **Brew House** Tab of the 3 Vessel sample. Every other Widget is added the same way.

> **⚠ A new Widget is made on the 🔨 Build Tab, not on your Tab.** It stays there until you press **✅ Finish Build ... > Move to Tab** (Step 8). Until then it is not on any of your Tabs. Do not stop after **💾 Save**.

Only an Admin can add a Widget.

## Step 1. Open Edit layout

Press **☰ Menu** (top right), then **✎ Edit layout**. The Edit layout bar opens above the Tab.

![☰ Menu: Edit layout](help_pictures/Step_Add_Device_01_Edit_Layout.png)

## Step 2. Pick the kind of Widget

On the Edit layout bar, pick the kind in **Select Widget Type** (here **Boolean Widgets (Switch, Slider, Checkbox, buttons, LED) and the Manual Valve**), then pick the Widget in **Add Widget** (here **Tap Water Valve (household hose bib Manual Valve Widget)**).

The **➕** buttons on the bar (**Picture ➕**, **Text ➕**, **Manual Valve ➕**, **Value ➕** ...) do the same in one click.

![Select Widget Type and Add Widget](help_pictures/Step_Add_Widget_02_Widget_Type.png)

## Step 3. It is made, and its Properties open

The **🔨 Build Tab** opens by itself, the Widget is made there, and its **Properties** open. A Widget has no Add box: there is no Port to pick, so it is made right away with a name like `wgt_TWV_tap_water_1`.

![The Hose Bib Widget Properties](help_pictures/Step_Add_Widget_03_Properties.png)

## Step 4. 1. Label

Type the **Label**: what you call it, in plain words, for example **Garden hose**. **Label Visible** shows the Label on the Tab; it starts off.

![1. Label](help_pictures/Step_Add_Widget_04_Label.png)

## Step 5. 3. Name

The **Suggested Name** under the Name follows the Label. When you change the Label, it goes in the **Change Name** box and a blue pop-up asks to change the Name to it: press **✏ Change Name** on the pop-up. Or type a name of your own in **Change Name** and press **✏ Change Name**. See [Interface, Tab and Device names](46-interface-tab-device-names).

![Change Name](help_pictures/Step_Add_Widget_05_Name.png)

## Step 6. 2. Tab, then Save

Leave **2. Tab** on **Build Tab** for now: you pick its Tab in Step 8. Look over the rest (Orientation, Hose or Pipe, Cover, Overlay, the IP Fittings, and where it sits); each one has Hover Help. Press **💾 Save**.

![2. Tab and Save](help_pictures/Step_Add_Widget_06_Save.png)

If you pick your Tab in **2. Tab** here instead, **💾 Save** sends it straight to that Tab, with no Finish Build.

## Step 7. It is on the Build Tab

The new Widget is on the 🔨 Build Tab (a Hose Bib is small: look for it). **👁 Preview** shows how it will look on Brew Day.

**It is not on Brew House yet.** Press **✅ Finish Build ...**.

![The Build Tab: Finish Build](help_pictures/Step_Add_Widget_07_Build_Tab.png)

## Step 8. ✅ Finish Build ... > Move to Tab

The **Finish Build** box asks where it should go. Under **Move to Tab**, pick the Tab (here **Brew House**) and press **Move to Tab**.

![Finish Build: Move to Tab](help_pictures/Step_Add_Widget_08_Finish_Build.png)

Everything on the Build Tab moves to that Tab, and the Build Tab closes. The other choices: **As a Template** (not for Widgets), **Throw it away**, **Keep building**.

## Step 9. Drag it to its place

No Tab was picked for it before, so it lands **in the middle of the Tab** and **glows red until you click it**. Drag it to its place.

![It lands in the middle of the Tab](help_pictures/Step_Add_Widget_09_Drag.png)

## Step 10. Save layout

Press **Save layout** to keep it.

![Save layout](help_pictures/Step_Add_Widget_10_Save_Layout.png)

## Step 11. Done

Press **Exit** to leave Edit layout. The Garden hose is on the Brew House Tab.

![The new Hose Bib on Brew House](help_pictures/Step_Add_Widget_11_Done.png)

What is left to do:

1. **Draw its hose or pipe**: **🛠 Build (Edit layout) > Draw hose** ([Pipes and hoses](24-pipes-and-hoses)).
2. **Change it any time**: in Edit layout, double-click it to open its Properties.

## If you stopped before Finish Build

An unfinished Build is never lost: it is kept on the Brain and comes back on the 🔨 Build Tab the next time you open **Edit layout**, with a note. Press **✅ Finish Build ...** to send it to its Tab, or **🗑 Discard Build** to throw it away. See [Saving never ends the Build](55-build-tab#saving-never-ends-the-build).
