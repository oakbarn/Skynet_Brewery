# The Menu Bar

The **Menu Bar** is the top line of the panel: the panel name, the **Schema** picker, the page buttons (Tabs, Processes, Variables, Log, Devices, Media, Import, Settings, Help ...), **Enable sound** and **Sign out**.

## Hidden on every Tab

When a Tab is showing (one of your screen tabs, or a Menu tab like Default), the Menu Bar is hidden, so the tab gets the room. This is handy on a phone.

- Press **☰ Menu** (top right) and pick **▼ Show Menu Bar** to bring it back.
- Press **☰ Menu** and pick **▲ Hide Menu Bar** to hide it again.
- Going to another page (Processes, Log, Settings ...) shows the Menu Bar there. When you come back to the Tabs page it is hidden again.
- In **Full screen** the Menu Bar stays hidden too. Press Esc or **Exit full screen** to leave full screen.

## Always showing the Menu Bar

An admin can switch it off in **☰ Menu > Default**: under **Menu Bar**, turn off **Hide the Menu Bar on Tabs** and press **Save**. Then the Menu Bar always shows, and the ☰ Menu has no Show / Hide Menu Bar choice.

## Alarm sounds

Browsers only play sounds after you press **Enable sound** once. It is in the Menu Bar, so pick **☰ Menu > Show Menu Bar** first, then press Enable sound.
