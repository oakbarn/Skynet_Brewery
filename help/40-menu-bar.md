# The Menu Bar

The **Menu Bar** is the row with your **Brewery Name** in the middle. At its right, always in the same place and the same order, on every Tab and in the Process Editor:

- **Simulator balloon** (yellow stripes): shows only in Simulator mode. Press it to go back to the real boards. **☰ Menu > Switch to Simulator** turns the Simulator on.
- **Build** (the hammer and wrench, admins): Edit layout on and off. It is not needed in the Process Editor, so it is hidden there.
- **Sound** (the ear with music notes): one click turns sound on, one more turns it off. A red **Do Not Enter** sign sits on its corner while sound is off. See [Alarms and sound](21-alarms-and-sound).
- **Emergency Off**: one click stops everything (see [Emergency Off](244-emergency-off)).
- The yellow **?**: Help.
- **☰ Menu**: every page and setting (see [The Menu](33-menu)).

An icon that is not needed now is hidden, but its space stays (it shows the background color), so the other icons never move.

**Undo, Save and Close** are blank until needed. On a Tab there is nothing to undo, save or close, so nothing shows. In Edit layout they are **↩ Put back**, **Save layout** and **Exit** on the Edit layout bar. In the Process Editor they are at the right of the second row.

## The Brewery Name

The middle of the Menu Bar shows your **Brewery Name** when you are a Patron (type it in **☰ Menu > Default settings**), and **Skynet Panel** when you are not. See [Patron](198-patron).

## The Top Line

Above the Menu Bar, the other pages have the **Top Line**: the panel name, the version, the **Schema** picker and the page buttons (Tabs, Processes, Variables, Log, Devices, Media, Import, Admin Settings, Help ...) and **Sign out**.

### Hidden on every Tab

When one of your Tabs is showing, the Top Line is hidden, so the Tab gets the room. This is handy on a phone. In the **Process Editor** it is off: every page is on the **☰ Menu**, and **Close** goes back to the Tabs.

Every **☰ Menu** tab (Zoom, Default, Equipment Profile, Schema, About ...) and every other page (Log, Settings, Help ...) shows the Top Line.

- Press **☰ Menu** (top right) and pick **▼ Show Top Line** to bring it back.
- Press **☰ Menu** and pick **▲ Hide Top Line** to hide it again.
- Going to a ☰ Menu tab or another page shows the Top Line there. When you come back to a Tab it is hidden again.
- In **Full screen** the Top Line stays hidden too. Press Esc or **Exit full screen** to leave full screen.

### Always showing the Top Line

An admin can switch it off in **☰ Menu > Default settings**: under **Top Line**, turn off **Hide the Top Line on Tabs** and press **Save**. Then the Top Line always shows on the Tabs, and the ☰ Menu has no Show / Hide Top Line choice.
