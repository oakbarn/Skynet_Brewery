//the Motorized out valves do the switching. The Kettle Manual Ball Valve on its Left Tangential (Kettle in) is opened the same way
//the first time wort goes to the kettle. The HLT in Ball Valve (HLT Right Tangential) is hosed to the Motorized To HLT valve on a Tee
