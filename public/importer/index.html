Welcome, BruControl brewer

This Importer turns your BruControl setup into a Skynet Schema, so your brewery comes up in Skynet with your boards, your Devices, your screens and your scripts. It only reads your BruControl files. BruControl and its files are never changed, so you can keep brewing with BruControl until you are ready.

In Skynet, everything you see and touch (the user interface) is in a browser: Microsoft Edge, Chrome, Firefox, DuckDuckGo, Safari and others.

The Skynet Panel is the brewery program. Its Brain can run on this Windows computer (or any computer with Windows 10 or newer), just like BruControl, or on a Mac or Linux computer, or on a Raspberry Pi. The choice of hardware is up to you, but to start, it can run right here, and you can move it later if you want.

We need your input, and some understanding of how things work in Skynet compared with BruControl. When something needs to be upgraded or changed, for example an Element that becomes a different type of Device in Skynet, we will explain why.

That is the first difference: where you added an Element in BruControl, you add a Device in Skynet. Let's look at the differences now.

BruControl and Skynet

What stays the same

  • Your boards, wiring and cables. The same Arduino Mega, Grand Central, ESP32 and friends, the same USB COM ports, Ethernet and WiFi links, and the same pin numbers. Nothing is rewired.
  • Your Devices. Pumps, valves, elements, probes and PWM outputs come over with their ports.
  • Your screens and scripts. Every workspace becomes a Tab, and every script becomes a Process that runs the same way.
  • Your pictures and sounds from the BruControl Media folder.

What is different in Skynet

  • It runs in a browser. The Brain (this PC, a mini PC or a Raspberry Pi) runs Skynet, and you reach it from any PC, Mac, tablet or phone.
  • Some new words:

  • Skynet pictures and Templates. Pumps, valves, pipes and vessels use Skynet's own drawings, with pipes that show the flow.
  • Everything comes in the Skynet way. Names, settings and script lines are changed to Skynet's words and style (for example a 0-255 output becomes 0-100%). Each change is listed for you.
  • Firmware. Each board gets Skynet firmware once, over its USB cable, from inside Skynet. No Arduino IDE.

How we will guide you

  1. Pick your BruControl config file. We look in Documents\BruControl first, or a folder you choose.
  2. Boards. We show each BruControl board and its COM port or address, so you can check them.
  3. Tabs and items, one question at a time. Each workspace becomes a Tab. For each BruControl item we ask you a few plain questions (is this a pump, a valve, a vessel? what kind?) so it becomes the right Skynet Device, Widget or Template.
  4. Processes. Your scripts are changed to Skynet Processes, and any line that needs a look is shown.
  5. Save the Schema and send it to your Skynet Brain, with a report of everything that changed.

Nothing is saved or changed until the last step, and you can go Back at any time.

Pick your BruControl config file

BruControl saves its whole setup in one config file that ends in .brucfg. Pick the one you want to bring into Skynet. One config file makes one Schema. If you have more than one, import each one on its own; they are never combined.
