# BruControl sounds: swap copyrighted ones for Skynet sounds (Importer only)

Status: **plan only.** The Importer is on hold until the Skynet Panel is done, so this sits in the Importer Sandbox (Rule 11.3)
until the Big Merge. No Importer code is changed. Plain-English list and risk ratings: [docs/Sound_Copyright_Check.md](../../docs/Sound_Copyright_Check.md).

## Why

The Importer copies the brewer's BruControl sounds onto the brewer's own Brain. Some of the OakBarn BruControl sounds belong to
record labels, film studios or Pixabay (docs/Media_Licenses.csv). They must never reach a Skynet sample or a Setup (Rule 9.12).
When a BruControl Alarm or Sound Player uses one of the sounds below, the Importer offers the Skynet sound in its place
(Interview question; Skynet sound first and Recommended, keep the brewer's own file below it). A sound not on this list copies as now.
The brewer's original files are never moved or deleted.
